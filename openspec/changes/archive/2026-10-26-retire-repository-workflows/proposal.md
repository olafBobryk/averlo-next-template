# Proposal

## Why

Repository implementation policy is still distributed across a special-purpose router and twenty concern documents. Move its remaining durable contracts into native OpenSpec capabilities, preserve structural and operational guidance at its owning sources, and retire the router without losing rules or requiring a replacement routing skill.

## What Changes

- Establish the remaining contracts as related, independently maintained capabilities. Preserve the existing form/loading specs unchanged and cross-reference shared lifecycle concerns instead of repeating their requirements.
- Give every clause in the current router and all twenty references a recorded destination: spec, existing owner contract, nearest AGENTS, canonical procedure, or explicitly justified duplicate retirement.
- Keep exact UI APIs, supported variants and visual defaults with Storybook; dependency direction and source topology with AGENTS; commands, source-intake steps and measurement with procedures and their existing operational skills.
- Preserve current exceptions, authorization boundaries, fixture-only behavior, provider decisions and approval requirements. This migration does not revive the deferred button-variant policy or authorize new product features, providers, thresholds or deployments.
- **BREAKING:** Remove the repository-workflows skill and its references after all destinations and dependent consumers are migrated. Update Compose, Animate, Systemize Composition, Contact Form, generated instructions, plugin documentation and router-dependent verification together. These operational skills remain available.
- Propagate applicable contracts and verification procedures into independent generated projects through positive assembly. Keep generator-only policy out of instance guidance.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `data-lifecycle/action-behavior`: non-form mutation ownership, conflicting actions, reversible optimism, confirmation and feedback lifetime.
- `interaction/keyboard-and-overlays`: accessible controls, composite navigation, focus, overlay stacking, dismissal and pending close locks.
- `interaction/responsive-and-motion`: primary-content availability across breakpoints and readable, interruptible motion/fallback states.
- `identity/access-and-membership`: server-resolved sessions, organization/platform boundaries, continuations, optional methods, invitations and identity safety.
- `identity/private-file-access`: authorized file lifecycle, opaque references and bounded access.
- `integration/api-transport`: consistent typed request outcomes, injected transport and secret separation.
- `delivery/submission-protection`: applicable server guards, neutral honeypots, file checks and policy-owned throttling.
- `delivery/contact-delivery`: configured server delivery, normalized recipient-independent messages, truthful results and privacy.
- `content/source-resolution`: static/guarded/authoritative source behavior and consistent normalized readback.
- `content/media-delivery`: durable constituent assets, source-neutral media, responsive delivery and alternative text.
- `content/marketing-composition`: ordered semantic content regions, shared shell ownership and stable comparison boundaries.
- `navigation/route-identity`: installed canonical destinations, semantic ancestry, contextual commands and denial behavior.
- `navigation/document-metadata`: family title/indexing policy and canonical/social/body source consistency.
- `experience/content-hierarchy`: coherent grouping, nonduplicated context and usable surface/action alignment.
- `ui-system/component-contracts`: discoverable supported owner contracts, complete variant teaching and faithful public projections.
- `domain/entity-presentation`: consistent entity facts/presentation across surfaces and authorized reversible lifecycle behavior.
- `template/project-assembly`: explicit output ownership, profile omissions and independent project materialization.

### Modified Capabilities

None. `data-lifecycle/form-behavior` and `data-lifecycle/loading-state-behavior` retain their complete requirement sections.

## Impact

Affected policy sources include `AGENTS.md` and nearest owners, `PRODUCT.md` ownership wording, `openspec/config.yaml`, `docs/testing`, `docs/operations`, `WORKFLOWS.md`, the Averlo plugin's dependent skills/documentation/tests, `verify-skill-references`, composition workflow tests, and positive assembly/generated instructions. Existing behavior verifiers remain authoritative executable evidence. Runtime repairs require a bounded approved scenario; this proposal does not authorize an application-wide redesign. No package upgrade, custom OpenSpec schema, routing index, installed plugin-cache mutation or plugin publication is included.
